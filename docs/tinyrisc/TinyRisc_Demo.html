What happens in each phase?

Fetch: read a word from instruction ROM into the instruction register. Execute: decode it, choose ALU inputs, and update the destination or control flow. RAM load: wait for synchronous memory data before completing the instruction. Most instructions take two clocks; RAM loads take three.

The accumulator in this program is R2, although the processor itself has a general register file. RAM word 10 holds the sum. ST to FF00 changes the LED latch. HALT retires and advances PC to address 10, then stops; no instruction is fetched there.
